Light-Painted Aircraft and More

Amazing light-painted photographs of aircraft, restaurants, vintage gas stations, famous landmarks, and a variety of unique, fun, and sometimes rare subjects. Many of these cool projects also feature more than one type of vehicle as well. In this unique photography gallery, you’ll find both civilian and military helicopters, jet aircraft, modern and vintage airplanes (including several experimental aircraft), a famous Route 66 landmark, as well as an amazing three-section Route 66 diorama. There’s even a light painting of “World-Famous” Betty’s Pies that was created under the Aurora Borealis on the north shore of Lake Superior! Also featured in this gallery are quite a few unique light paintings that include people (pilots, high school seniors, and kids) in the final imagery. Many of these unusual portrait light paintings include a car, an aircraft, and even a cool drum set! In most cases, you'll see both the setup photo and the completed artworks. The difference between the two versions is amazing! If you would like to discover more information about any project, simply click on the button under the light-painted photograph. From there, you’ll be taken to the full story found on our sister website's blog at Michael Anderson Photography (www.adesignerportraits.com). Thank you, and have a great day! - M&J
