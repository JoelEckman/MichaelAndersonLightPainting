Light-Painted Heavy Construction Equipment

Unique light-painted photographs of heavy construction equipment. Also in this unique gallery are images of commercial logging equipment, which are pretty big rigs themselves! Each of these cool images is preceded by its "setup" photo, usually, but not always, taken before sunset. Then, once it is dark enough, the rigs are photographed using dozens of exposures (sometimes hundreds), with the lighting in each one being applied to just one small area at a time and from multiple angles. In post-production, Michael takes the best parts of those images to create a Master File that is full of amazing detail! Michael typically writes stories about each light painting project that they create. You can find those stories on our sister website’s blog at Michael Anderson Photography (www.adesignerportraits.com). There’s a button under each finished artwork that will take you directly to the unique backstory of each project. We appreciate that you're here visiting our website, checking things out. Thank you! - M&J
