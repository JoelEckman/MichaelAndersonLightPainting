Light-Painted Corvettes

Fabulous Corvette photography, all light-painted by professional photographers Michael and Joannie Anderson. They’ve light-painted so many of these fine automobiles that they have this entire page dedicated to Corvettes! Here, you’ll find Corvettes from pretty much every era, from C1’s to C8’s. To date, Michael and Joannie have created light paintings of more than 60 Corvettes! The growing list includes one or more from the following years: ’57, ’58, ’60, '63, ’64, ’65, ’66, '67, ’69, ’77, ’93, '02, ’04, '06, '12, ’13, '14, '15, ’16, ’17, ’19, '23, '24, and 2025! In this gallery, you'll typically see the setup photo (whenever possible), followed by the completed one-of-a-kind light-painted artwork. The difference between the before and after imagery is amazing! By clicking on the button below each final light painting, you’ll be taken to "The Rest of the Story" about each project found on our sister website's blog at Michael Anderson Photography (www.adesignerportraits.com). Thank you for taking the time out of your day to enjoy our images! - M&J
