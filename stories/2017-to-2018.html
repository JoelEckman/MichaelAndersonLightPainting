Light Painting Stories, 2017 to 2018

Featured below are the finished artworks, as well as the interesting mini-backstories, from all of the light painting projects that Joannie and I created during our first year and a half of light painting. Every one of these one-of-a-kind light-painted images has a very unique story that brought them to fruition. Use the buttons below the images to take you to "The Rest of the Story" on our sister website's blog at Michael Anderson Photography (www.adesignerportraits.com). These light painting projects are in chronological order, beginning with our very first light painting created in May of 2017, all the way through the end of 2018. We hope that you enjoy reading about each project, and we thank you for taking the time to check them out! - M&J

Light-Painting Coca-Cola ProductsIn the spring of 2017, I set out for a week-long photography class with fellow professional photographer John Hartman. The class was all about a photography technique called light painting.Little did I know at the time, but this would be a career-changing class for me! Upon returning home from John's class, I was on a mission to light paint just about anything I could (just ask Joannie)!This is the very first light painting project that I ever photographed. It was created in class, right there in John's studio in Stevens Point, Wisconsin. My photography would never be the same!

The Story About the Coca-Cola Light Painting

Light-Painting a Cool Bar Remodeling Done by Julkowski Inc.I was fresh out of my "Light Painting 101" class, and I needed to practice this stuff before I forgot what I had learned!So, as many people will do when they need help, I turned to my family. Our daughter Jen and her husband, Ben, had just remodeled their entire basement. They added a wonderful bar area next to their new family room.Their remodeling project was designed and built by Julkowski Inc., and they did a fantastic job!!I never wrote a blog post about this light painting project; however, I did put together a "Build Video" of it on our YouTube Channel. Clicking on the button below will take you there.

The Julkowski Inc. Bar Remodel "Build Video"

Light-Painted Wedding Photography at Olympic Hills Golf ClubUpon returning from John's class, I thought that if I kept on practicing this new skill, I might become proficient at it. So, since Joannie and I were professional wedding photographers, why not see if light painting could work at a wedding?Derek & Zlata's wedding was only a couple of weeks after I had returned. I'd already light-painted the bar remodel, so why not give it a try at a wedding? Our newlyweds were excited that they got to be our guinea pigs!Despite a rainy evening and lots of mistakes made, it is still a really pretty wedding photograph! The old phrase, "Nothing ventured, nothing gained!" could not have been truer!It turned out that we were just barely getting started!

Olympic Hills Wedding Light Painting Story

Light-Painting a 1977 Classic White Corvette named "Snow White"This was my very first "solo" light painting of an automobile, a "Classic White" 1977 Corvette. What I found out afterward is that all-white and all-black vehicles are the most difficult to light paint. Why start with something easy?A good friend of ours, Gary, let me "borrow" the car, nicknamed "Snow White", so that I could practice with her. These images were created right in our very own Portrait Park, which is part of our home-based studio.I was starting to get the hang of this "light painting" thing!

The 77 Corvette Light Painting Backstory

Light-Painting of a 1955 Chevrolet BelAirJust one week after light painting Gary's Corvette, I was light painting a 1955 Chevrolet BelAir in historic Stillwater, Minnesota.The famous Stillwater Lift Bridge that you see in the background was about to be closed to vehicular traffic forever. Now, after a full refurbishment, it is a pedestrian and bike bridge as part of a 4.7-mile loop that goes into Wisconsin for a while, and back across the new Highway 36 bridge, and then back to Stillwater.

1955 Chevy BelAir Light Painting Story

Light Painting a 1938 Chevy CoupeThe very night after I light-painted the BelAir, I had another cool light painting project! This time, it was a 1938 Chevy Coupe! This was an interesting project to light paint, especially since it was only my third-ever light painting of a car (with all 3 of them created in just over a week)! This car's stunning paint job, with hand-painted lightning bolts, was a real challenge to light paint. It changes color with different angles of view! "Isn't that paint job cool!" exclaimed Ron, the car's owner. To which I replied, "Yes, but I'm trying to make it look like it's all the same color!"

Light Painting a 1938 Chevy Coupe

Saint Paul College Club Wedding Light PaintingThis time, we were better prepared to tackle a light painting at a wedding! I had learned an awful lot in a little over a month! And, unknown to Joannie and me at the time, we were creating images that no one else, anywhere in the world, was creating at weddings!And, if I don't say so myself, we kind of knocked this one out of the park at Clint & Gretchen's Saint Paul College Club wedding!

Saint Paul College Club Wedding Light Painting Story

Light Painting New Brighton Fire Engine #5New Brighton Fire Engine #5 was the very first fire truck that I ever light-painted. My many years as the professional photographer for the New Brighton Department of Public Safety paid dividends here. I asked them if I could try something new with one of their rigs, and they gave me the go-ahead.It turned out to be great timing, as long-time firefighter, Ron Finney, Sr., was about to retire. So, as a surprise to Ron, we added him to the light painting in post-production. They presented the finished metal print to him as a surprise gift at his retirement party! Ron was photographed, on a different night, in one of the bays at the station. All we told him was that I was "trying something new." And that wasn't a lie in any way, shape, or form!

The Light Painting of New Brighton's Fire Engine #5
