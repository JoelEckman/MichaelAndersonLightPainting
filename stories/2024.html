Light Painting Stories, 2024

Featured on this page are all of our 2024 light-painted artworks, as well as their interesting mini-backstories. Joannie and I were truly blessed with some really amazing subjects to light-paint in 2024! We’ve written a full article about each project on our sister website’s blog at Michael Anderson Photography. These articles talk about what we did to take the subject from being just an ordinary photo to a really cool light-painted photograph. When you click on the button below the images, it will take you to "The Rest of the Story”. As a side note, most of those stories include other photographs, and many also include the setup shot taken prior to the light painting getting underway. There’s also a link to the “Build Video” for each project. Those videos, found on our YouTube channel, feature the images that were used to create the final artwork. Then, you’ll see the entire light-painting come to life, one layer at a time, in post-production. These 2024 light-painting projects are in chronological order. We hope that you enjoy viewing and reading about them, and we do thank you for taking the time to check them out! - M&J

Drag the line across any photo with a setup shot to see the scene before it was light painted.

Light Painting a 2023 Z06 Corvette - Version One

Once again in 2024, I traveled to Missouri for the Lake of the Ozarks Corvette Show. These guys put on such an amazing show each year, and I'm always excited to return! The first light painting project that I had lined up was this amazing 2023 Z06 Corvette! The owners of this amazing car, Mike and Sandy, know the owner of this beautiful location that overlooks Lake of the Ozarks. Besides the spectacular view from here, the land owner has the actual flying saucer from the Hollywood hit movie, "Men in Black"! Besides being in this image, you'll see another one featuring that flying saucer shortly.

Light Painting a 2023 Z06 Corvette - Version Two

As I was setting up for the previous light painting featuring the front two-thirds view of the car, I thought it might be cool to do a second light painting showcasing the rear two-thirds view of this amazing Z06 Corvette. I had multiple cameras and two tripods along, so why not? I asked Mike and Sandy if they liked the idea of a second version of the whole car, and they did! Not only did we create the two full-body versions of their stunning Corvette Z06, but we also did five more light paintings that featured parts of their Z06! We did both the front and rear end views, the "frunk", the amazing interior, and the engine compartment too! See below for those unique light paintings.

Light Painting a 1958 C1 Corvette

Light Painting a 1977 Pontiac Firebird

I saw this stunning 1977 Pontiac Firebird at Motorhead Madness in March of 2024, in Duluth, Minnesota. Ron & Nikki, owners of North Speed & Restoration in East Bethel, Minnesota, had it on display there. Their company did the restoration, and they knocked it out of the park! After talking to them at the show, I just had to create a light painting of this beautiful car! Ron and I light-painted this one on a beautiful May evening and had the finished artwork completed in time for their Open House in early June. It's now on display in their lobby.

Light Painting the Howard 500 N500LN

This is the Howard 500 N500LN! This amazing aircraft is one of only 17 that were ever built. It's also one of only two remaining that are still flight-worthy! Joannie, our new friend Craig, and I light-painted this beautiful aircraft on a spectacular night in late summer. This was our third attempt, as the weather did not want to cooperate the first two times. Mother Nature apologized by giving us an amazing sunset that night!

Light Painting our 2024 "Storytime with Santa" Set
