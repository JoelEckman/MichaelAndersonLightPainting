Light Painting Stories, 2021 & 2022

This page on our website is a fun little recap of the light painting projects that we created in 2021 and 2022. COVID was making it difficult to be as busy as we wanted to be, but the projects that we did have were pretty darn amazing! Weddings even made a little bit of a comeback, but nothing like they were pre-COVID. Each one of these projects starts off with a mini-story about it, followed by the final light-painted artwork. Then, the button under each of the images is linked to “The Rest of the Story”, which is found on our sister website at Michael Anderson Photography. To discover more about any particular image, just click on that button below the photograph, and you'll be there! Many of those stories include other photographs, with most including the setup shot taken prior to the light painting getting underway. Nearly all of them include a link to the “Build Video” for each project that’s found on our YouTube channel. The Build Video features the images that Michael used to create the final artwork. Then, as it goes along, you’ll see the light painting come to life, one layer at a time, in post-production. Most of these videos are short, usually about one-minute long.

Drag the line across any photo with a setup shot to see the scene before it was light painted.
