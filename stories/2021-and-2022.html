Light Painting Stories, 2021 & 2022

This page on our website is a fun little recap of the light painting projects that we created in 2021 and 2022. COVID was making it difficult to be as busy as we wanted to be, but the projects that we did have were pretty darn amazing! Weddings even made a little bit of a comeback, but nothing like they were pre-COVID. Each one of these projects starts off with a mini-story about it, followed by the final light-painted artwork. Then, the button under each of the images is linked to “The Rest of the Story”, which is found on our sister website at Michael Anderson Photography. To discover more about any particular image, just click on that button below the photograph, and you'll be there! Many of those stories include other photographs, with most including the setup shot taken prior to the light painting getting underway. Nearly all of them include a link to the “Build Video” for each project that’s found on our YouTube channel. The Build Video features the images that Michael used to create the final artwork. Then, as it goes along, you’ll see the light painting come to life, one layer at a time, in post-production. Most of these videos are short, usually about one-minute long.

Light Painted Wedding Photograph at Golden Valley Country ClubAs we mentioned above, due to COVID, weddings didn't make much of a comeback in 2021. However, the few weddings that we did get the opportunity to photograph were really fantastic!Alex and Alyssa's pretty wedding at St. Phillip the Deacon in Plymouth, Minnesota, was our first of the year (and it was already May). Their wedding day was awesome, culminating in this pretty light-painted wedding photo created at Golden Valley Country Club!

Wedding Light Painting at Golden Valley Country Club

Light Painting Wedding Photography at the JX Event Venue in Stillwater, MNApril and Dillon's wedding day in Stillwater, Minnesota, was incredible! Between the venue, our cute couple, the crazy (in a good way) wedding party, and fabulous families, this wedding truly was a treat to photograph!Their wedding ceremony and their beautiful reception were held at the beautiful JX Event Venue in downtown Stillwater, Minnesota. Creating their beautiful light-painted wedding photograph on the Grand Staircase was the whipped cream and cherry on top!We would be returning to Stillwater the very next day for another fun wedding! Tina and Ryan's wedding would be held on the Jubilee II Paddleboat! That's coming up next!

Wedding Light Painting Photography at JX Event Venue

Light Painting a 1953 Ford Crestline VictoriaAs we mentioned in the previous light-painted wedding photo for April and Dillon, we photographed Ryan and Tina's wedding the very next day. Ironically, also in downtown Stillwater, Minnesota!However, Ryan and Tina's wedding light painting would be created on a different night from their wedding for a few good reasons.Their fun wedding was on the Jubilee II Paddleboat, so there's not much room to navigate (pardon the pun) onboard. But the main reason that we delayed their light painting was that theirs would include a car. And, not just any car, but a wonderful 1953 Ford Crestline Victoria that has been in the family for many years!Light painting a car properly takes at least three hours, or more, and there's simply not enough time on a wedding day for that. It was also Memorial Day Weekend. Downtown Stillwater was way too crowded! So, we created this cool light-painted wedding photo a couple of weeks later on a nice, quiet weeknight in Stillwater!

Wedding Light Painting with a 1953 Ford Crestline Victoria

Light Painting a 2017 C7 CorvetteIn June, I returned to Stevens Point, Wisconsin, for another light painting class with a bunch of John's former students in attendance. The focus of this class was to share ideas and tips on light painting. John always lines up some fun light painting projects that we can do as a class. The caveat is that we cannot share the images on the internet. So, you'll have to ask us to see those when we meet.However, this particular year, one of the photographers in attendance, Don, drove there in his 2017 Corvette! Well, we gotta light paint that! So we did!Since we students did this project all on our own (kind of like "extra credit"), I can post this one!

Light Painting a 2017 Corvette

Light Painting Five Construction VehiclesIt was late summer, and the big construction project was still going strong near our daughter Jen's home. I had already created some cool images of heavy equipment used on-site the previous year. (Those are all right here on our website on the "Light Painting Stories - 2020" page.)Well, this group of bulldozers and excavators was getting parked like this for several nights in a row. So, of course, I had to light-paint them!I texted a photo to Jen to show her where I was that night, and about two minutes later, our grandkids, Riley and Ryder, were there in their pajamas to say hi to Grandpa!  If you check out the backstory on this one, you'll see that the setup photo has Ryder in it wearing his Batman pajamas!I have two versions of this light painting! After the pretty sunset, the moon and stars became the second one.These rigs are owned and operated by North Pine Aggregate in Forest Lake, MN.

Light Painting Five Construction Vehicles

Wedding Photography Light Painting at a Lake Elmo Event CenterMatt and Grace's wedding was more than a bit different (in a good way)! They actually got married twice! And both times, it was to each other! The first time was in 2020, and then again, one year later in 2021.Once again, it was a COVID thing.Wedding #1 was with just a small group of family and friends. Joannie and I were there too, and we photographed their wedding at Lake Elmo Event Center under all of the COVID restrictions that were in place at the time. Despite all that, those wedding images really turned out wonderful!Well, one year later, they got to do it all over again! Except that this time, everyone else could come to celebrate with them! Because this was a luncheon affair, this would become our second-ever light-painted wedding photograph that was created outdoors in broad daylight! Pretty cool!
