You’re part of making it

It’s important that you’re there for the shoot. Your input decides the final location, how your subject is positioned and the camera angle, and there’s a good chance you’ll lend a hand during the session too.

Clients tell us again and again that being part of it was the best part. You’ll see exactly how much skill goes into the artwork on your wall.

How a commission works

  1. Get in touch

    Call, text or send the form below. Near the Twin Cities, we can meet at your location. Out of town, a phone call gets it started.

  2. Plan the shot

    We look at the location with you, often earlier on the day itself, to find the best angle and the right time.

  3. The light painting night

    Usually three to four hours, sometimes more, depending on the size of your subject and the setting.

  4. Review your artwork

    Your one-of-a-kind light painting is ready to see 10 to 14 working days after the session.

  5. Hang it on your wall

    Prints on aluminum are ready in about two to three weeks.
