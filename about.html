Some Little Tidbits About Michael & Joannie

Joannie and I have owned and operated Michael Anderson Photography in Mounds View, Minnesota, since 1986. We began creating light-painted photographs in 2017.

Over the years, we’ve studied photography with some of the who’s who of photography, including John Hartman, Ben Shirk, Bruce Hudson, Hanson Fong, Don Blair, Mike Colón, Denis Craft, Clay Blackmore, Denis Reggie, Angela Carson, Monte Zucker, Bambi Cantrell, Ed Pierce, Gary Fong, Eddie Tapp, Scott Kelby, and many, many others.

In addition to our light painting photography, we're award-winning wedding photographers as well. We're the only wedding photographers in the world who create light paintings of whole scenes at weddings.

We’ve been selected for numerous awards for our wedding photography, including The Knot's "Best of Weddings” more than 15 times. And we were inducted into The Knot Hall of Fame in 2015.

We've also received the coveted Wedding Wire "Couples' Choice" Award six times. And Expertise has selected us as one of the "The 21 Best Wedding Photographers in Minneapolis" seven times.

But that's not all. We also create wonderful family, kids, pets, and high school senior portraits, both on location and at our home-based studio. We often incorporate light painting into our high school senior, engagement, pets, and kids portrait sessions, too!

Anderson's Portrait Park is our very own outdoor location at our home-based studio for portrait creation as well. And yes, we've light-painted vehicles in our Portrait Park!

Light painting has brought out a new creative edge in our work. Our clients are really loving it, and you will too!
