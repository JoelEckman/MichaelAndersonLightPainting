Light-Painted Emergency Vehicles

Cool light-painted photographs of police cars, firetrucks, and other emergency vehicles.

We even have three unique light-painted photographs of perhaps the coolest crime-fighting vehicle of all-time - The Batmobile!

In this gallery of light-painted art, you’ll see what the scene looked like prior to light painting it. It’s followed by the unique one-of-a-kind light painting of some pretty awesome and hard-working emergency vehicles. Pretty cool!

In addition, some of the light-painted photographs also include police officers, firefighters, engaged couples, and even kids!

We’ve written stories about each of these cool projects. To learn more about any of them, just click on the button below the finished artwork, and you’ll be taken to the full story on our sister website’s blog at Michael Anderson Photography. (www.adesignerportraits.com)

Thank you! -M&J
