Light Painting at Weddings

Stunning, one-of-a-kind light-painted wedding photographs that were produced in the dark at weddings.

Each of these wedding photographs was created at an actual wedding (not staged) by professional wedding photographers Michael and Joannie Anderson, from Mounds View, Minnesota.

Michael and Joannie are the only wedding photographers in the world who produce these full-scene light paintings at weddings.

These unique images are created by taking dozens of photographs in the dark. In each image, a small area is lit during each exposure. Then, Michael selects the best parts of those images to create an amazing one-of-a-kind wedding artwork!

Use the buttons below the completed artworks to take you to "The Rest of the Story" on our sister website's blog at Michael Anderson Photography (www.adesignerportraits.com).
