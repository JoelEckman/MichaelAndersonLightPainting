Cars, Trucks, and Motorcycles

Amazing light-painted automotive photography created by Mounds View, Minnesota, photographers, Michael and Joannie Anderson.

In this unique gallery, you'll usually (but not always) find the "before" photos, followed by the one-of-a-kind light-painted photographs of fabulous cars, awesome trucks of all sizes, and several that include pretty cool motorcycles. The difference between the two versions is quite literally “day and night” (pun intended)!

Many of these projects include the client’s pretty homes and businesses. Others include barns, abandoned airports, State Parks, lakes, vintage garages, and other cool locations all over the country.

The vehicles in this amazing collection range in age from a cool 1915 Ford Model T to vehicles that are brand-new! And, as mentioned, it’s not just cars and trucks. There are a fair number of Harley-Davidson motorcycles mixed in there, too!

Please use the buttons below each completed light painting to take you to "The Rest of the Story" about each project that’s found on our sister website's blog at Michael Anderson Photography (www.adesignerportraits.com).

Thank you, and enjoy!
