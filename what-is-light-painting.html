What Is Light Painting Photography?

Light painting photography is essentially applying light to a subject in a manner that enhances its detail. It’s a unique photographic process that is usually created in the dark. In its simplest form, light painting can be created in a single exposure. However, Joannie and I have developed a light painting photography method that really pushes the envelope! Our unique light painting style involves lighting everything we possibly can in the entire scene, not just the main subject. And, we do this from multiple angles. We typically do so in very small increments. By combining dozens, and sometimes hundreds, of those exposures, we can capture an amazing amount of detail that is truly remarkable! After many hours of post-production, the final Master File is then printed on aluminum, which is the icing on the cake! These fabulous artworks will look amazing on display in your home or office (or both)! Years ago, we started creating these one-of-a-kind light-painted images at the weddings that we photographed. Because of all that experience, we’re experts at adding people to our light-painted photographs. We’ve even added dogs and race horses to some of them! When people (or pets) are added, it gives you the option of having your artwork two ways - both with, and without you, in it. That makes for a very unique Christmas card, and very cool one-of-a-kind gifts for family and friends. Much more about this unique photographic process is described below.

As mentioned above, light painting photography is a unique and complex photographic process that is almost always created in the dark. Michael and Joannie’s method utilizes specially modified LED lights and powerful high-speed strobe lights to create a series of images of a stationary subject.
Once the subject is placed, neither it nor the camera can be moved until all of the exposures have been made. With each exposure, Michael will light small portions of the image from different angles to bring out the details of the main subject. Then, he will do the same thing, all over again, to light the rest of the scene as well.
They will often make 250 to 400 images (sometimes many more!) during the actual photography session. The whole photographic process typically takes about 3 to 4 hours for one vehicle and the scene.
Michael then takes the best of those images, usually about 100 or so, and combines them in post-production. The entire photographic process creates an amazing one-of-a-kind, ultra-high-definition image.
Post-production typically takes anywhere from 12 to 18 hours, and sometimes much longer! It all depends on the number of vehicles and the size and scope of the entire scene.
This unusual photographic technique highlights the subject’s details in a way that is simply not possible with any other photography method.
As we mentioned at the top, the icing on the cake is when we print your finished light-painted artwork on aluminum! It's done through a sublimation process. Your finished image is infused onto a coated aluminum substrate and looks absolutely stunning!
Michael is one of a very select group of photographers worldwide to employ this type of image creation. He's studied this very specialized photographic technique for many years and has perfected a light painting style that's all his own.
