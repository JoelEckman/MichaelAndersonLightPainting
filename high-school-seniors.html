Light Painting Projects Created for High School Seniors

These are light painting projects that were created specifically for some of our High School Seniors.

Most of these one-of-a-kind light-painted images include a vehicle of some sort. Whether it was their vehicle or someone in the family owned it, they got to use it as a “prop” for an amazing photo opportunity during their senior year!

There are even a few light painting projects here that don’t even have a vehicle of any kind, just a great prop or location!

In this senior photo gallery, you’ll usually find the "before" photo. It’s then followed by the one-of-a-kind light-painted senior portrait, many times, with a fantastic light painting of a vehicle!

Occasionally, we’ll even add our senior to a scene that’s already been light-painted. These senior portrait composites are pretty cool!

In most cases, Michael will write a story about the project and include some of the seniors’ traditional senior photos in addition to the light painting project.

Use the buttons below the completed artworks to take you to "The Rest of the Story" on our sister website's blog at Michael Anderson Photography (www.adesignerportraits.com).

Thank you, and have a great day! - M&J
