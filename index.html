How a light painting is made

  1. Set up before sunset

    The camera goes on a tripod and stays put. A setup photo records the scene as it really looks.

  2. Light it in the dark

    Once it’s dark, they make 250 to 400 exposures, often more, each lighting one small area from a different angle.

  3. Build the master file

    Michael takes the best of every exposure and blends them into one image, full of detail no single photo could hold.

  4. Print it on aluminum

    The finished artwork is printed on aluminum, ready for your home, office, hangar or showroom.

Meet Michael and Joannie

Michael and Joannie Anderson have owned and run Michael Anderson Photography in Mounds View, Minnesota, since 1986. They’re a husband-and-wife team who work side by side on every project, out in the dark, one exposure at a time.

They began light painting in 2017 and have since done more than 60 Corvettes alone, plus aircraft, fire engines, construction equipment, seniors and wedding couples. They’re award-winning wedding photographers, inducted into The Knot Hall of Fame, and the only ones anywhere who light paint whole scenes at weddings.
